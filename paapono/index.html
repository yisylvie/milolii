Improving the Lives of Our Miloliʻi ʻOhana

Paʻa Pono Miloliʻi strives to preserve and protect the cultural, historical, environmental, and archaeological life and heritage within the ahupuaʻa of Hoʻokena, Pāpā, Hoʻōpūloa, Miloliʻi, Omokaʻa, Kalihi, Honomalino, Okoe, and Kapuʻa.

Discover Our Projects

Paʻa Pono is dedicated to supporting our Miloliʻi community through our many projects and events.

  • Office of Hawaiian Affairs Culture Grant

    Perpetuating the Hawaiian cultural practices of Miloliʻi

  • Miloliʻi Community Center

    Establishing a central gathering place for meetings, celebrations, and cultural protocol

  • ʻŌpelu Project

    Enhancing the marine and coastal knowledge base and stewardship action of Miloliʻi’s keiki
