Our Core Values

ʻOnipaʻa in our manaʻo since 1980!

  • ALOHA—love, affection, compassion, sympathy

    Let us love and respect our environment, our village, our kūpuna, and one another.

  • ʻIMI ʻIKE—to seek wisdom, enlightenment and education

    Let us seek knowledge and enlightenment.

  • MĀLAMA—to care for, to protect, to maintain

    We care for our family, our classmates, our teammates, our friends, and ourselves.

  • ʻIKE PONO—to know, to see, to feel, to understand, to comprehend, and to recognize.

  • KULEANA—privilege, responsibility, right

    We each have a personal responsibility to our ocean, our village, our canoes, and our equipment.

  • HOʻOMAU—to preserve, to perpetuate, to continue

    Let us perpetuate the values of our ancestors, our kūpuna, and our responsibility to one another.

  • HAʻAHAʻA—humility

    Let us live our daily lives with humility in everything we do; we serve others before we serve ourselves.

  • LŌKAHI—oneness, unity, harmony

    We are our strongest when we are united together in perfect harmony.

  • LAULIMA—working together

    Many hands work together for everyone’s success.

  • ʻIHI—Respect

    Let us respect ourselves, our kumu, our kūpuna, our ʻohana, and all living things.

  • ʻONIPAʻA—steadfast

    Like our organization name “Paʻa Pono,” we stand for what is right. We hold firm to our beliefs and our traditions.

  • KŌKUA—help

    If you see someone in need—a family member, a friend, a kupuna, or even a stranger—stop and lend a helping hand.

  • MAHALO I KA MEA LOAʻA—Appreciate what you have.

    Appreciate your kūpuna, your parents, your hale, your health, your ocean, your land, and your friends.

  • HOʻOHANOHANO—dignified, honorable, distinguished, prestige

    Conduct yourself with honor and dignity at all times. Walk with your poʻo held high, and be a proud but humble Native Hawaiian!
